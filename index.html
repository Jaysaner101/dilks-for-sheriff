Why I'm Running

23 Years of Service

Two decades-plus serving Indian River County, and a U.S. Army veteran before that. I know this job from the ground up — there's no learning curve, just proven experience.

Accountability & Transparency

Facebook comments go back on. The community deserves a voice — even when it isn't flattering. No more silence, and no more hiding from the people this office serves.

An End to Favoritism

The days of special treatment and looking the other way are over. The law applies the same to everyone in this county — no insiders, no exceptions, no politics.

A Voice for the Community

This office answers to the people of Indian River County — not to a party, a PAC, or the powerful. I'll show up, listen, and always tell you straight.
