Contribution Information

  • Contributions to a candidate for a countywide office in Florida are limited to $1,000 per donor, per election. The primary and general are separate elections.
  • Cash contributions are limited to $50. Contributions are not tax-deductible.
  • Florida law requires the campaign to record each contributor's name, address, and occupation (and employer). You'll be asked for these on the secure form.
  • By contributing, you certify the funds are your own and that the contribution is permissible under Florida law.
